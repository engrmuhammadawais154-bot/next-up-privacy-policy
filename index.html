Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 03, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Next Up mobile application ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google or email, we collect your email address, name, and basic profile information to authenticate your account and sync your data.
  • App Data: The tasks, projects, and spaces you create are stored securely in our cloud database (Firebase) to allow cross-device syncing and sharing with other users (if you use collaborative spaces).
  • Calendar Data: The app requests permission to read and write to your device's calendar. This is solely used locally to sync your tasks with your calendar. We do not transmit your device's calendar events to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app's functionality.
  • To authenticate users and securely sync data across devices.
  • To send you push notifications and alarms for tasks and reminders that you set.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The primary third-party services we use are:

  • Google Play Services: For core Android functionality.
  • Firebase (Google): For authentication (Firebase Auth) and cloud data storage (Cloud Firestore).

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party service providers.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Your tasks and data are private to you, except when you explicitly choose to share them with other users by inviting them to a shared "Space".

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at our support email address.
